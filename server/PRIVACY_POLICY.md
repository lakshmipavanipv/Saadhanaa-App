# Body & Soul Ring — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 June 2026 · Operated by Velvue · Contact: saadhanaring@velvue.in_

> ⚠️ This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a lawyer
> for compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP)
> and the DPDP Rules before you publish/launch. Host it at
> `https://velvue.in/body-soul-privacy` (the URL the app links to).

## 1. Who we are
Body & Soul Ring ("the app") is operated by Velvue ("we", "us"). We are the
Data Fiduciary for the personal data described below.

## 2. What we collect (only with your consent)
- **Identity & contact:** name, email, phone (from Google sign-in or manual entry).
- **Account & device:** sign-in method, device model, app version, timestamps.
- **Usage & diagnostics:** screens visited, features used, session frequency, crashes, issues you report.
- **Spiritual practice:** chosen deities, japa counts & rate, sadhana / meditation / yoga minutes.
- **Health & wellness (sensitive, optional):** heart rate, HRV, SpO₂, steps, calories, sleep from your ring/phone. Collected only if you opt in.

## 3. Why we use it (purposes)
To provide and operate the app; personalise your AI plans and insights; improve
the app; produce anonymised/aggregated analytics; and respond to your support
requests. We process this data on the basis of **your consent**.

## 4. We do NOT
- Sell your personal data.
- Use it for purposes beyond those listed without fresh consent.

## 5. Storage, security & location
Data is stored on our secured server (Velvue, hosted in India) and authenticated
via Google Firebase sign-in. We apply reasonable technical and organisational
safeguards. Data is retained until you delete your account, after which it is
erased (see rights below).

## 6. Your rights (DPDP Act, 2023)
- **Access** a copy of your data (in-app: Settings → Privacy → Export).
- **Correct** inaccurate data.
- **Erase** your account and data (in-app: Settings → Privacy → Delete account).
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (in-app: Settings → Privacy); collection stops immediately.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights.
- **Grievance redressal** — contact our Grievance Officer below.

## 7. Children
You must be 18 or older to use the app, or have verifiable consent from a
parent/guardian. We do not knowingly collect data from children without such consent.

## 8. Grievance Officer
Email: **saadhanaring@velvue.in** — we respond to grievances within the
timelines required by law.

## 9. Changes
We will update this policy and re-request consent if the data we collect or our
purposes change.
